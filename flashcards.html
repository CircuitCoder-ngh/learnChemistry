Flashcards

Flip a card, answer honestly, and let the boxes decide what you see next. Cards you know wait longer. Cards you miss come right back.

    How the boxes work

    This is the Leitner system, an old trick that still beats rereading. Pulling an answer out of your head is what makes it stick, and waiting a little longer each time makes it stick harder.

    Your boxes live in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data clears them.
