How to read it

Every tile tells you the same few things. The one you will use most on exams is the atomic mass, because it doubles as the molar mass in grams per mole.

Brackets, as in [222], mean the element has no stable isotopes, so the number is the mass of one long-lived isotope. Masses come from PubChem, so the last digit can differ from your textbook's table.
